KUIS Corpus Explorer

A research tool for the KUIS Indonesian learner corpus. Group annotated files into corpora, then run frequency, collocation, n-gram and concordance analyses across any selection of them — reading either the learner text as written or the corrected text.

What it does

  • Word frequency & collocationsRanked counts with per-million normalisation, filterable and exportable as CSV.
  • N-gramsRecurring 2- to 5-word sequences, counted within each file.
  • Concordance (KWIC)Every occurrence of a word or phrase in context, sortable by either side.
  • Original vs corrected textSwitch between the text as written and the corrections recorded on each annotated segment.
